## Overview

This folder holds the live-session webinar delivered on May 6, 2026: **"Agentic AI: Eight Insights for
Business Leaders."** It's a 12-slide capstone briefing — eight leadership-facing "insights," each framed
as a question a leader should be asking their organization, followed by concrete guidance on how to
proceed. The through-line for the whole session: *"The difference between a language model that
generates text and an agent that acts in the world is not a matter of scale, but of scaffolding."*

## Why This Session Matters

Where Modules 1–3 build the conceptual, architectural, and governance foundations of agentic AI, this
session compresses the entire program into eight decision-forcing questions a leader can bring straight
back to their own organization. It's designed as the synthesis moment — the eight insights map directly
onto the program's own arc (see below), so it also works as a structured recap.

## The Eight Insights

The deck groups the eight insights into four thematic pairs:

**Insights 1–3 — What agentic AI actually is**
1. **Beware the Seduction of the Demo** — a working demo is not a working deployment; map corner
   cases, hallucinations, and error propagation before scaling, and don't let an impressive prototype
   drive resourcing decisions on its own.
2. **The Workflow, Not the Task, is the Unit of Value** — value is lost in the handoffs *between* tasks,
   not in the tasks themselves; redesigning the workflow's choreography is a leadership decision, not a
   technology one.
3. **Autonomy is a Dial, Not a Switch** — different agents need different autonomy settings, calibrated
   by stakes and reversibility (not enthusiasm); treat "autonomy creep" as a silent risk that should only
   ever expand by deliberate decision.

**Insights 4–5 — How to evaluate and govern it**
4. **Evaluate Against the Current Process, Not Against Perfection** — benchmark agents against the
   honest, documented baseline they're replacing (error rates, cycle time, audit gaps), not an imagined
   ideal; value traceability and consistency alongside raw accuracy.
5. **Architect Safety into the System Around the Model** — critical constraints belong in application
   logic, not prompts, because a model can talk its way past a prompt-level rule; layer hard-coded
   constraints, output verification, reversibility-by-design, and code-verified escalation paths.

**Insights 6–7 — What changing the organization requires**
6. **The Binding Constraint is Organisational, Not Technical** — scaling agentic AI touches identity, and
   your most capable people may become your biggest source of resistance; pair top-down sponsorship
   with bottom-up experimentation, and distinguish "AI-first" (in the strategy deck) from "AI-native"
   (the actual default way people work).
7. **Compressed Cycles are Discipline, Not Recklessness** — a six-month evaluation cycle is itself a
   risk in a fast-moving field; adopt a three-week MVP discipline (pilot with a handful of users, learn,
   then scale) instead of marrying a vendor whose features will be obsolete in months.

**Insight 8 — Where the strategic horizon is heading**
8. **Methodology is Becoming the New Source Code** — playbooks, frameworks, and decision rules are
   candidate intellectual assets to encode as agentic workflows; companies built around a methodology
   are quietly becoming software companies, so invest in appreciating assets (structured knowledge,
   clean data, documented process) over depreciating ones (rigid custom code, tight vendor lock-in).

## Key Takeaways

* The session is structured as eight leader-facing questions, not eight technical explainers — it's meant
  to be brought back into the room, not just absorbed.
* Scaffolding (workflow design, evaluation discipline, architecture, organizational readiness) is the
  recurring theme — not model capability.
* The closing framing ties every insight back to a single sentence: agentic AI is a scaffolding problem,
  not a scale problem.
